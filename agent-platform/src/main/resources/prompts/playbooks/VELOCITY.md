Rule: many transactions in a short window.

- Many small card-not-present payments to unfamiliar merchants in quick succession suggest card testing:
  stolen card details being checked before larger fraud.
- Many outgoing transfers in quick succession suggest account takeover or a mule account.
- A burst at familiar merchants with ordinary amounts (e.g. a shopping trip) is often legitimate.
- Check whether the merchants or payees are new, the amounts, the channel, and what followed the burst.
- Typical actions: block the card for card testing; for transfers, contact the customer and hold
  further outgoing transfers.
- Regulations: search only when transfers are involved (loss responsibility, suspicious transaction
  reporting).
